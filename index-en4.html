SUMMARY

How to Distinguish the Genuine — What This Site Conveys

1

An Official Status Recognized by the Government

In 1874 (Meiji 7), the state recognized the Hashimoto family as shizoku (samurai class). This is proven by official documents bearing the seal of a government official.

2

Contemporary Diaries That Cannot Be Falsified

The Hashimoto family name appears in primary sources — such as a diary from Kofukuji Temple dated 1496 — written on the spot by third parties. There is no room for later fabrication.

3

The Danger of "Authority Without Evidence"

Using an unsubstantiated lineage as a "mark of prestige" in elections or commerce carries legal risks of misleading consumers. This site aims to correct such practices.
